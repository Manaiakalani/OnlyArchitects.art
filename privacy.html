Privacy policy

Draft. A lawyer has not reviewed this.

OnlyArchitects credits the people who design and build a house. It does not show who owns the house, what it sold for, or photos of the inside from a public record.

On this iPhone the app can keep saved buildings, asks, claims, flags, notification choices, people you follow, the home you checked, messages, process posts, boards, bids, content reports, and blocked people. Delete account removes that file, the sign-in session in the Keychain, and asks the project to remove inquiries you sent. An inquiry is stored for the person you chose. It is not emailed. Messages, posts, boards, and bids are not sent. Feedback opens Mail and is not stored. Report removes that item on this iPhone. Block hides that person's messages and posts on this iPhone. Emailing a report is optional and does not include the item text or a street address. A camera look reads a photo you choose and does not save it.

The camera is used only while a scan is open. Location is used only while a scan is open, or while you turn location on for the map. Notification permission is asked only if you turn a notification on. No notification is sent.

Public-record names come from permits, the Washington historic inventory, and Wikidata. Each public-record credit shows its source and the date it was retrieved.

Data the app can send

Collected data is used for app functionality only. It is not used for tracking, analytics, advertising, or product personalization.

  • Precise location is read only while a scan is open, and only while map "Near me" is on. It is not linked to you and it is not sent with an inquiry.
  • The sign-in email stays on this iPhone. An inquiry does not upload it. The message field is where the sender writes a way to reply.
  • Sign in with Apple keeps its user id on this iPhone. Sending an inquiry stores a random id from this iPhone so delete account can ask the project to remove those rows.
  • An inquiry stores the project type, budget band, timeline, lot status, and message for the person chosen in the app. It is not emailed.

Name, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, payment info, search history, and coarse location are not collected for an account. A claim photo and Camera look read an image the person chooses and do not save it. The catalog does not store owner names or sale history.
